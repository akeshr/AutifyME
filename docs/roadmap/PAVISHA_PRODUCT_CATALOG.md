## Overview

PAVISHA manufactures food-grade PET packaging products across 7 core product families. This document structures their catalog for systematic product onboarding into the database.

**Core Focus:**
- PET containers (bottles, jars, cans, preforms)
- Specialized bottles (fridge bottles, kids water bottles)
- Complementary closures and covers for PET products
- Does NOT manufacture sealing machines or straws

**Certifications:**
- BPA-free materials
- Food-grade certified
- Food-safe packaging

## Product Families

### 1. PET Bottles
**Category:** Rigid Containers
**Primary Use:** Beverages, liquid foods, household products
**Target Industries:** Beverage manufacturing, food processing, FMCG

**Common Variant Axes:**
- **Capacity:** 200ml, 250ml, 300ml, 500ml, 750ml, 1L, 1.5L, 2L
- **Neck Finish:** 28mm PCO, 38mm PCO, 48mm (for wide-mouth applications)
- **Color:** Clear (transparent), Amber, Blue tint, Green tint
- **Shape:** Round, Square, Custom (for branding)

**Typical Applications:**
- Carbonated soft drinks (soda, energy drinks)
- Packaged drinking water
- Fruit juices and beverages
- Cooking oils
- Liquid detergents and cleaners

**Base Specifications:**
- Material: Virgin PET resin
- Weight range: 18g-45g (varies by capacity)
- Thread type: ISBT/PCO standards
- Barrier properties: Standard (gas/light)

## Database Mapping Strategy

Each product family will map to the database schema as follows:

### ProductFamily Record
- `name`: "500ml PET Bottle"
- `sku_prefix`: "PAV-BTL-500"
- `category_id`: Link to "Rigid Containers > Bottles"
- `google_product_category`: "Home & Garden > Kitchen & Dining > Food Storage > Food Storage Containers"
- `base_price`: Base price before variant adjustments
- `custom_attributes`: Material specs, certifications, weight

### VariantAxis Records (per family)
- Capacity (500ml, 1L, 1.5L)
- Neck Finish (28mm PCO, 38mm PCO)
- Color (Clear, Amber, Blue)

### VariantValue Records (per axis)
- Each specific value with SKU code for generation
- Price adjustments (if any)

### Product Records (SKUs)
- Generated via Cartesian product: 3 capacities × 2 neck finishes × 3 colors = 18 SKUs
- Each SKU: `PAV-BTL-500-CLR-28PCO`, `PAV-BTL-500-AMB-38PCO`, etc.
